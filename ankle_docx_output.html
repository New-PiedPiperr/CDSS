    2. No

    1. Yes

TESTS

Special Test for Recurrent Lateral Instability

  1. Talar tilt test: The ankle is held in the neutral position, examiner stabilised the tibia just above the ankle and then tries to force the ankle into maximum inversion. The ROM can be measured or compared with the contralateral side.
    Inversion laxity suggests injury to calcaneofibular and anterior talofibular ligaments (ATFL).
  2. Anterior Drawer Test: Patient sits with knee flexed at 90 deg and ankle in plantarflexion of about 10 deg. The lower leg is stabilised by the examiner and the force patient’s heel forward over the tibia. If positive, the talus can be felt sliding forwards and backwards. Laxity indicates tear of the ATFL.
