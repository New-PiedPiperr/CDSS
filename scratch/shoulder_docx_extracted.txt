P: SPECIAL CONFIRMATORY TESTS
P: Test for Cervical Radiculitis: 
P: Spurling’s Test
P: While pt is seated, the examiner applies side flexion of the neck towards the affected side while applying axial compression.Sign is positive if the pain sensation felt in the shoulder-neck region is replicated.
P: Tests for SIS
P: The painful arc: Ask pt to Abduct arm across range of motion actively
P: Scapular rhythm is disturbed
P: Pain is aggraavated between the range of 60 - 120 deg
P: Pain is alleviated IF shoulder is laterally rotated on ABduction
P: If positive, SIS is indicated
P: Neer Impingement Sign: Stabilise the scapula with one hand and raise the unaffected side to full flexion with Abduction and internal rotation passively.
P: The test is positive when there is pain at the subacromial space.
P: Rule out acromioclavicular osteoarthritis, glenohumeral instability and SLAP lesions.
P: Hawkins-Kennedy Test: Pt arm is placed in 90 deg of elevation at the scapular level and 90 deg flexion; Stabilise the upper arm with one hand and internally rotate with the other hand.
P: The test is positive when pain is felt in the anterolateral aspect of the shoulder
P: Jobe’s test: Elevate arm in scapular plane, extend elbow and thumb points towards floor with arm internally rotated. Pt is asked to hold the position with downward pressure from examiner
P: Positve sign = Pain on examiner restriction indicating supraspinatus irritation.
P: Tests for Isolated Muscle Weakness
P: The ‘Empty Can Test’ for Supraspinatus: Patient is asked to ABduct up to 90 deg, 30 deg flexion and internal rotation. Examiner stands behind patient and applies downward pressure
P: Positive sign, Affected side is weaker than the unaffected side.
P: Positive sign suggests tear in the supraspinatus
P: Infraspinatus Test: Pt holds arms close to body, elbow is flexed up to 90 deg of forward elevation in scapular plane. External rotation is instructed while examiner applies resistance.
P: Lack of power on one side indicates weakness of the infraspinatus muscle
P: If test is done with arm in 90 deg elevation
P: i. Inability to resist external rotation suggests a tear of the infraspinatus
P: ii. Movement with feelings of pain indicates an irritation to the infraspinatus
P: Subscapularis (Belly Press Test): Pt is asked to place their hand on their abdomen and move elbow forward. Pt is then asked to press hand on the abdomen.
P: If subscapularis is torn, Pt will be unable to perform movement and elbow will move backwards.
P: Lag SignFor External Rotation: Pt lifts arm slightly from body and is asked to hold in external rotation.
P: Positive when hand recoils to neutral position and suggests tear of infraspinatus or supraspinatus
P: Drop Sign. The examiner places the arm at 90 deg ABduction, elbow at Rt angle and arm maximally rotated.
P: Positive sign, arm drops from position. Suggests a tear of the infraspinatus OR the posterior part of the rotator cuff
P: The Lift-Off Test (Subscapularis): Pt is asked to place hand on the back at the mid-lumbar spine.The therapist then lifts hand from the back and pt is asked to hold it there
P: Positive sign, inability to maintain hand while removed suggests subscapularis weakness.

P: Special Tests for Recurrent Subluxation
P: Apprehension Test:
P: Patient is in sitting or lying position, the examiner lifts the arn gently into ABduction, external rotation and then extension. Test is positive if Pt senses that humeral head is about to slip out anteriorly and body stiffens in apprehension.
P: The test is repeated with the anterior part of shoulder protected.
P: Fulcrum’s Test:
P: Pt lies supine, arm ABducted up to 90 deg. The examiner places their hands over the shoulder to set the fulcrum base. The humeral head is levered forward by extending and externally rotating arm. The patient immediately becomes apprehensive.
P: Drawer’s Test:
P: Patient lies supine , scapula is stabilised with one hand while the upper arm is grasped firmly with the other, and manoeuvring is done forward and backward (like a drawer). The drawer’s test is positive if instability is marked.
